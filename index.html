TETRIS

HOW TO PLAY

←→
Move the piece sideways. Hold to slide.
↑XZ
Rotate clockwise (↑ or X) or counterclockwise (Z). Pieces kick off walls.
↓
Soft drop: fall faster, 1 point per cell.
Space
Hard drop: land instantly, 2 points per cell.
C
Hold: swap with the stored piece, once per piece.
NEXT
The panel on the right shows the next three pieces.
Ghost
The outlined piece shows where yours will land.
Lines
Fill a row to clear it. Single 100, Double 300, Triple 500, Tetris 800 (× level). Combos and back-to-back Tetrises earn bonuses. Every 10 lines raises the level and speed.
PR
Pause / restart.
